# One Take guides

Start with the [front page](../README.md) if you have not set the app up yet. Then read whichever guide answers your question.

**Using the app**

1. [Recording](recording.md): load your slides, set up the mic and the speech line, choose Auto or Press to speak, record your camera, draw on the slides, fix a mistake, and the keyboard shortcuts.
2. [Preview and editing](editing.md): listen back, reorder, and change the lecture clip by clip.
3. [Exporting](exporting.md): where the video goes, formats, quality, compression, streaming, speed, filler words, subtitles, and the list of your exports.
4. [The studio](studio.md): the deep clean and keeping only your voice, with models on the local server.
5. [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md): when something is not working.

**Understanding it**

6. [How recording works](how-recording-works.md): what counts as speech, the noise filter, who is speaking, the pen under the hood, what is saved where, privacy.
7. [How exporting works](how-exporting-works.md): the export stages, large exports, the system check, the hardware encoder and the performance modes, and how speed, filler words and transcription are done.

**Running and changing it**

8. [The server and its API](server.md): starting the server, its options, where files go, security, and every HTTP endpoint.
9. [Development](development.md): the project layout, scripts, tests, the end-to-end runs, browser support and the design system.
